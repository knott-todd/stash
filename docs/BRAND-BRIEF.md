# Loft — brand brief (signed 2026-11-07)

Source of truth for every screen and every line of copy. If an element doesn't serve this, it goes.

## Mission
Storage companies hook you with a free tier, let it fill, then charge rent. Loft is storage that is **free, unlimited and private**, and stays that way.

## Who
A phone-first person, 25–45, not technical. Met us at the "storage full" popup. On a phone, in a hurry, wary: "free + unlimited" sounds like a scam.

## Promise
**Free. Unlimited. Private.** (this order). Everything on the page exists to make those three believable in under five seconds.

## Feeling after setup
Relief. "Handled. I never think about this again."

## Personality
The quiet product. Not a character. An object that works. The brand's job is to get out of the way.

## References
Apple, Arc, Nothing: space, one thing at a time, few words, no decoration, the product shown rather than described.

## Design principles
1. Every element proves one of the three words, or it goes. No feature cards, no savings calculator, no jokes, no metaphors.
2. Say it once. One headline, at most one sentence of support, one button.
3. The product is the picture. No illustrated or built "hero object"; the only picture is the real app, one tap away.
4. Space is the brand. "Unlimited" is expressed as emptiness and calm.
5. Trust lives in details: precise type, exact alignment, system-level honest language. No marketing adjectives.
6. Mobile is the design; desktop is the adaptation.

## Product truth
"Private" is earned by **encryption before storage**: files are encrypted in the browser (WebCrypto, AES-GCM) before upload, so the storage supplier only ever holds ciphertext. The key belongs to the account and is held by Stash, so sign in works anywhere and sign out is clean. Say this plainly: "only you, signed in, can open your files", never "we cannot read your files". Decision: Olusanya, 2026-11-07 option A; revised 2026-11-08 to accounts.

## Mechanism stays backstage
Users don't need to know how Loft works. Telegram is not named on the landing page; inside the product it's named only where the user has to act on it (the "Open Telegram" step).

## Voice rules
- Second person, present tense, short declaratives.
- No exclamation marks. No "seamless", "effortless", "powerful", "beautiful".
- Numbers over adjectives ("0 passwords", not "no hassle").
- System language is allowed and preferred ("Encrypted on your phone before it leaves").
